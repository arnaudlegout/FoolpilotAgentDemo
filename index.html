AI Agent Study — Interactive Demos

Try the two AI-assistance conditions used in our study of how AI suggestions influence the answers people give and how confident they feel. Each condition reproduces one of the two dominant LLM interaction formats, injecting correct or wrong suggestions.

🧪 These are demonstration pages, not the live study. They are the real experiment interfaces with data collection removed: nothing you type is saved, transmitted, or recorded — there is no reCAPTCHA, no questionnaire server, and no participant tracking. Nothing leaves your browser. Reload a page to restart it.
AUTOCOMPLETE

Inline autocomplete

You write your own answer, and the agent proposes a completion inline as you type. Press TAB to accept the suggestion, or ignore it and keep typing.

 CHATBOT

Conversational chatbot

You ask a built-in chatbot and read its reply, then write your own answer. The reply is a deterministic, scripted suggestion — the same manipulation as the autocomplete condition.
Study conducted by INRIA and LAPCOS (Université Côte d'Azur, France).
