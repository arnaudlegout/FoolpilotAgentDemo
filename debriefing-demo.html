Thank you for your participation!

We thank you for your participation and invite you to carefully read the following information, which will help you better understand the study you took part in and its objectives.

As previously mentioned, the goal of this study is to better understand the subtle influence of AI on human responses. Specifically, we aim to investigate how AI-generated suggestions can shape users' answers — sometimes without their conscious awareness. We hypothesize that this influence may vary depending on how the AI's response is presented or the difficulty of the question.

To explore this, you were asked to answer questions of varying difficulty, each with the help of an AI agent. Your responses will help us gain a deeper understanding of AI's influence and usage, to raise awareness among users about the potential consequences of using AI.

If you were unsure about certain answers, found some questions difficult, or realized afterward that the AI may have influenced your response — this is entirely expected.

We hope that this brief explanation has clarified the objectives of the study.

The data will be analyzed collectively, not individually. All study data is stored on a password-protected external hard drive.

We also kindly ask that you do not share details about this study with others who may participate, so that everyone can experience it under the same conditions.
